Time Attack+ Point System & Rules ►

Goal: Hit active (Red) targets as fast as possible.

  • Base Hit: +1 Point
  • Reaction Bonus:
      ≤ 0.6s: +3
      0.6s–0.9s: +2
      0.9s–1.4s: +1
  • Combo Bonus: +5 Points every 3 consecutive hits.

Penalties:

  • Miss (Inactive Node): -1 to -5 Points (based on difficulty).
  • Combo Reset: Occurs on Miss OR >4s without a hit.
